## Hypothesis

Persistent positive primary-market net creations across the complete universe of
U.S. spot Bitcoin ETPs may represent regulated allocator demand and Bitcoin
inventory absorption. If source feasibility is later proven, Aurex may preregister
one weekly BTCUSDT Spot long-or-cash candidate using aggregate net-creation pressure
and cross-fund breadth. Missing, late, revised, ambiguous, or narrowly concentrated
fund observations must select cash, never a short position.

This mechanism is distinct from all exhausted Aurex families. It excludes price
patterns, volatility, order-book or aggressor flow, funding and basis, macro,
Bitcoin on-chain settlement, stablecoins, options, CFTC positioning, and ETP
secondary-market prices or volumes.

## Required Causal Reconstruction

The 2024 ETP approvals used cash creations and redemptions. The SEC permitted
in-kind processing on `2025-07-29`, but a regulatory authorization date does not
prove that every fund began operating in kind on that date. S4 must establish each
fund's actual effective regime from official filings and issuer records.

Before any ETP numeric value may be read, a frozen metadata protocol must prove:

1. The complete SEC-approved U.S. spot Bitcoin ETP universe and lifecycle history.
2. First-trade, closure, merger, reorganization, and ticker-change dates.
3. Availability of complete daily shares-outstanding history for every fund.
4. Availability of daily Bitcoin holdings or Bitcoin-per-share history.
5. Actual publication timestamps or causally defensible availability markers.
6. Per-fund cash-only and in-kind operational effective dates.
7. Creation basket, fee, split, and non-creation holding-change reconciliation.
8. Survivorship-safe calendar construction and explicit missingness handling.
9. Zero-dollar access and documented reuse rights for all required sources.

Failure of any gate must stop S4 before numeric data, parameters, or returns.
