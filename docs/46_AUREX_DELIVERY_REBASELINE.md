## Native Spot Risk Closure - 2026-09-29

Resumed the interrupted September 27 implementation. This supersedes the prior
next step to implement cost-aware sizing. The local native spot policy now covers

Implemented:

- `src/aurex/risk_engine/native_spot.py`: BTCUSDT LONG only, explicit 100-unit
  starting capital, paper mode, USD/USDT model units, and one open position across
  the account. Existing open trades or positions block another entry, even if the
  legacy profile allows more. Orders without a corresponding trade also block
  admission because their account ownership and exposure cannot be established.
- Shared cost arithmetic in `src/aurex/execution_engine/costs.py`, used by risk and
  paper fills. Quantity rounds down at internal ledger precision; entry and
  stop-exit commissions/spread/impact and monetary rounding are budgeted. The
  tighter of modeled stop risk and available cash determines size. The native
  cost floor is 10 bps commission plus 2.5 bps each half-spread and impact per leg.
- Native risk fractions cannot exceed 0.5% per trade, 1% per UTC day and 3% per UTC
  week. The risk base is the lesser of current and starting equity. Remaining
  daily/weekly budgets are reduced by negative net realized P&L before admission.
  Cumulative net strategy and account loss limits are each 10 units from initial
  capital, not trailing peak drawdown. Positive P&L does not expand these budgets.
  Near-exhausted budgets reduce size; exhausted budgets reject the next entry.
- Reward:risk is checked after costs, with loss rounded conservatively upward
  and reward downward. Price precision/range must survive ledger persistence.
  Incomplete/future-dated closed trades or equity that fails reconciliation block
  admission. Decision snapshots contain prices, quantity, budgets, realized P&L,
  cash required, cost assumptions and explicit limitations.
- Paper execution checks that a decision belongs to its signal, and native
  execution re-evaluates its approval before creating an order. Changed costs,
  prices, quantity, equity, kill state, exposure or missing sizing evidence cannot
  silently reuse the earlier approval. Legacy replay and imported evidence were
  not rewritten to adopt the new native sizing semantics.

The native policy is intentionally single-position. It does not claim general
multi-position cash reservation. A modeled stop budget is not a guaranteed loss
ceiling: the gap test records the larger actual simulated loss and blocks subsequent
entries at the loss limit. Binance tick/lot/minimum-notional validation remains a
separate prerequisite before exchange-bound execution. USD/USDT parity is a stated
paper assumption, not a currency guarantee. No strategy has become eligible merely
because sizing tests pass.
